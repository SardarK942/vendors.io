## What this doc is (and isn't)

**Is:** the actual scripts to send this week, the follow-up cadence, the reply playbook, the tracking sheet columns, and the funnel we're measuring.

**Isn't:** a marketing strategy deck. There is no fluff. Every line here is meant to be executed as-is or copy-pasted verbatim.

---

## The frame — why this pitch works

Before any script, agree on the story. Every DM leans on these four claims. All four are true today:

1. **Founding vendor / early access.** Baazar is a new cultural-wedding marketplace launching in Chicago. Vendors we onboard now become the visible roster when couples arrive — meaning better placement than late joiners.
2. **5% only, and only when you book.** We take a 5% deposit through Stripe when a couple books. That's our fee. You keep 95% and settle the balance directly with the couple. No monthly fee, no lead-buy, no commission on inquiries.
3. **Cultural-wedding niche.** Baazar is only for cultural weddings — South Asian, Nigerian, Persian, Filipino, mixed-heritage. Every inquiry a vendor receives is from a couple already looking for exactly what they do.
4. **They quote how they already quote.** Packages if they have them, custom quotes if they don't. The product supports both. No new workflow to learn.

The frame we _don't_ lean on: **"we'll send you leads next week."** We can't promise volume yet. Overpromising here poisons trust the moment they log in. Founding-vendor language is honest; leads-language is not.

## The universal 4-line script

Every DM has the same shape. Only line 3 changes per category.

```
[Line 1 — Personalization] Loved your [SPECIFIC thing — a real reel, a real
photo, a real detail]. [Optional: one-sentence why.]

[Line 2 — Who we are] I'm Sardar, founder of Baazar (baazar.io) — a new
cultural-wedding marketplace launching in Chicago.

[Line 3 — The hook, category-specific] See variants below.

[Line 4 — Ask permission, not a click] Cool if I share a bit more about how
it works? No pressure either way.
```

### Rules for line 1 (non-negotiable)

- **Reference something they actually posted.** Not "your work is amazing" — name the specific reel, photo, or venue. If you can't do this because their feed is thin, skip them.
- **Under 15 words.** Longer reads as a bot.
- **No emojis.** One is fine if it matches how they post. Zero is safer.

### Rules for line 4 (non-negotiable)

- **Do NOT paste a link in message 1.** Instagram down-ranks DMs with links. Also reads as spam.
- **Ask for permission.** "Cool if I share more?" invites a two-way conversation. "Check us out at [link]" is a one-way pitch that dies unread.

## Category variants — line 3 only

Pick the vendor's category, drop in the matching line 3. Everything else stays the same.

### V1 — Photobooth / DJ / MUA / Videographer (package-native)

> We're building the founding vendor roster in Chicago before we open to couples. You'd get first placement + your existing packages listed exactly how you already sell them. We only take 5% when a booking closes — nothing otherwise.

### V2 — Photographer

> We're onboarding a founding photographer cohort in Chicago before we open to couples. You'd list starting prices and every couple sends you the full brief (date, venue, hours, extras) with their inquiry — so you can quote real numbers on message one. 5% deposit through us when they book, you keep 95%.

### V3 — Caterer / Venue / Large decor (custom-quote native)

> We're onboarding a founding vendor cohort in Chicago before we open to couples. No packages needed on your end — couples send you a quote request with headcount, cuisine, and event details, you send back your real quote in your CRM inbox. 5% deposit through us when they book, you keep 95%.

### V4 — Mehndi artists / Small decor / Content creators (long-tail fallback)

> We're building a founding vendor roster in Chicago before we open to couples. Your listing is free, you get inquiries directly, and we only take 5% when a booking closes. Zero upfront cost, zero monthly fee.

## The follow-up cadence — 2 additional touches, same thread

Send in the same DM thread. Do not open a new one.

### Follow-up 1 — day +3, if no reply

> Hey, saw you were slammed [or: probably lost in your DMs]. Wanted to circle back — we're only onboarding [10-15] founding vendors in your category before Chicago opens up. Worth a 60-second peek?

### Follow-up 2 — day +7, if still no reply

> Last note from me — no worries if it's not the right fit. If you ever want to check it out, we're at baazar.io. Wishing you a solid rest of the season either way.

**That's it. Three touches max. After follow-up 2, mark as "closed — no reply" in the sheet and never DM again from the same account.**

## Reply playbook — what to say when they respond

### "Sure, tell me more" / "How does it work?"

> Short version:
>
> 1. You take 3 minutes to claim your listing at baazar.io/vendors/[their-slug-if-scraped]
> 2. Couples browse, filter by category + culture, and send you an inquiry with all their event details
> 3. You reply with your quote in your Baazar inbox — accept, adjust, or decline
> 4. When they accept, they pay a 5% deposit through Baazar. That's the only fee — no monthly, no commission on the 95%. You handle the rest directly with them.
>
> Want me to send the direct link to your listing?

Only send the link _after_ they say yes to that question. Two reasons: the link now feels earned, and IG's throttle doesn't count against you.

### "What does it cost?"

> Free to list. We take 5% of the booking as a Stripe deposit only when a couple books. That's it. No monthly fee, no lead-buy, no commission on the 95% you collect directly.

### "Do I need to make packages? I quote every event custom."

> Nope — the product supports both. Vendors who quote custom just get a "Request quote" button on their profile. Couples send their event details (date, headcount, whatever's relevant), and you reply with your real quote in your inbox. Same as your Google form + WhatsApp flow now, just cleaner.

### "How many couples do you have?"

> Being upfront — we're new. That's why we're onboarding founding vendors first. You'd be in the roster before couples arrive, which means top placement when they do. If lead volume is your top concern today, this isn't that yet — but if you want to be in early on a cultural-wedding-only platform, this is the time.

### "How is this different from The Knot / WeddingWire / Zola?"

> Three things:
>
> 1. Cultural-only — every couple on Baazar is looking for a cultural wedding vendor. No wasted inquiries from couples who wanted "generic Instagram wedding."
> 2. 5% deposit, no monthly fee, no ad-buy. Their model is $200-800/mo to be listed. Ours is $0 to list.
> 3. You keep 95% of the booking direct — we're not a middle-man on payments, just the intro.

### "Not interested"

> Totally get it — thanks for the honest reply. If it changes, you know where to find us. Wishing you a great season.

Then **mark the row "declined" + reason** and never message from the same account again.

### "Send me the link"

> Here's your listing — you can claim it in about 3 min: baazar.io/vendors/[their-slug]

(If they weren't scraped, send them to baazar.io/join-vendor or the equivalent onboarding start URL.)

## The tracking sheet — build this before the first DM

One row per vendor. Google Sheet, Airtable, or Notion — pick whichever is fastest for you.

**Columns:**

| Column                 | Type             | Notes                                                                             |
| ---------------------- | ---------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `vendor_name`          | text             | Business name                                                                     |
| `ig_handle`            | text             | @handle without the @                                                             |
| `category`             | enum             | photo / video / mua / dj / photobooth / catering / venue / decor / mehndi / other |
| `city`                 | text             | Chicago default, but log specifically (Naperville, Aurora, etc.)                  |
| `script_variant`       | enum             | V1 / V2 / V3 / V4                                                                 |
| `personalization_used` | text             | The specific thing you referenced in line 1 (proves you personalized)             |
| `sent_at`              | date             | Date of DM #1                                                                     |
| `read`                 | yes/no           | IG shows "Seen" — check daily                                                     |
| `replied`              | yes/no           |                                                                                   |
| `reply_sentiment`      | enum             | positive / neutral / question / negative                                          |
| `followup_1_sent`      | date             |                                                                                   |
| `followup_2_sent`      | date             |                                                                                   |
| `link_sent`            | date             | When you sent the direct listing link                                             |
| `link_clicked`         | yes/no           | Check `scraped_vendor_engagement` table (or GA) for clicks on their slug          |
| `claim_started`        | yes/no           | Did they hit /claim? Check DB                                                     |
| `wizard_step_reached`  | enum             | basics / location / online / details / portfolio / review / published / none      |
| `marketplace_visible`  | yes/no           | Final success = onboarding_complete=true AND is_active=true                       |
| `drop_reason`          | enum + free text | packages / friction / no_time / not_interested / no_reply / other                 |
| `notes`                | text             | Anything worth remembering                                                        |

**Why every column matters:** each one lights up a specific funnel gate. If `replied=yes` but `link_clicked=no`, the pitch worked but the landing page didn't. If `link_clicked=yes` but `claim_started=no`, the profile didn't sell the claim. If `claim_started=yes` but `wizard_step_reached=basics`, we lost them in the first two minutes of onboarding. Every drop-off is a clue.

## Guardrails — the hard rules

### Do

- **Warm the IG account before you start.** If the account has under 100 followers, no bio, no posts, IG will action-block you within 20 DMs. Post 3–5 things about Baazar, follow ~50 real cultural-wedding accounts, get a few interactions. Then start DMing.
- **Personalize line 1 every single time.** No exceptions.
- **Reply within 2 hours during business hours.** A cold DM that turns into a real conversation dies if you take 24 hours to respond.
- **Log every send in the sheet the moment you send it.** If you batch this, you'll lose data.
- **Switch to their website contact form or email if their IG is clearly dead** (no post in 3+ months, no story activity). Don't waste a send.

### Don't

- **Don't exceed 25 DMs from one account per day, ever.** Start at 15/day for the first week; ramp to 25 in week 2 if you get no action-blocks.
- **Don't paste a link in message #1.**
- **Don't message accounts with under 1k followers unless they're clearly a real business.** Small accounts sometimes flag your IG as spam, and one flag drops you into the request tab of every future recipient.
- **Don't send between 11 PM – 7 AM their local time.** Reads as bot.
- **Don't message the same vendor from two different accounts.** They will notice.
- **Don't promise leads.** Don't say "you'll get bookings" or "we have X couples waiting." Both are false today. Founding vendor / early access is the honest frame.

## Weekly funnel review — Sunday, 30 minutes

Every Sunday, spend 30 minutes on the sheet:

1. **Compute funnel percentages:** sent → read %, read → replied %, replied → link-clicked %, clicked → claim-started %, claim-started → marketplace-visible %.
2. **Rank the leaks.** The lowest-percentage transition is what you fix next week.
3. **Which variant is winning?** Reply rate by V1/V2/V3/V4. If one is dramatically underperforming, rewrite line 3 for that variant next week.
4. **Which categories are converting past claim?** If photobooth vendors are marketplace-visible at 40% and photographers are at 5%, the wizard has a photographer-specific issue — read those vendors' profiles + reach out for feedback.
5. **One change per week.** Don't rewrite 4 things at once. You need to isolate what moved.

Write down the change you're testing next week in a `weekly_notes.md` alongside the sheet.

## What we're NOT doing in v1 (parked, revisit only if data demands)

- Multi-account IG sending — one account only until we prove the pitch
- Email outreach — no reliable emails on the list yet
- Paid ads to vendors — no budget until we know CAC
- Vendor-referral bonus — premature until we have vendors to refer
- Category-specific inquiry form questions — parked until wizard drop-off data shows the current form is the bottleneck
- Wizard UX overhaul beyond the copy PR shipping alongside this doc
- Instrumentation dashboard — Sheet is enough for the first 100 vendors

Everything above is a real future improvement. None of it blocks Monday.
